0.10.2 keeps the flight list for flights, and makes updates finish where you can see them.

## Changed

- **No more empty recordings**: a recording that holds under 10 seconds, or never left the sim's menu position, is no longer kept. Ending one from the panel now says there was nothing to save instead of "Flight saved". Recordings you already have are left as they are; delete them from the list if you like.
- **The window opens after an update**: FOQA-Sim starts in the tray, so after an update it used to reopen out of sight. Now it opens its window, and the update card says "Installing… FOQA-Sim will restart" before the installer takes over.
- **Flights in the header**: the flight list has its own button next to Trends and Challenges.
- **The version is shown** next to the name; click it for what's new.
