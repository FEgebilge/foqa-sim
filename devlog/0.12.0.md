## Challenge starts
- **The start holds itself until you take it.** The app holds the challenge's speed with the throttle and level flight with the pitch trim. Move the throttle and it is yours; move the yoke or the trim and the trim is yours. Both are yours at the gate at the latest, so everything scored is flown by hand.
- **Mixture and trim start sensible**: full rich and a light nose-up trim, not wherever you last left them.
- **Weather and time are part of the challenge.** A challenge can set the sky (clear to thunderstorm, or snow), its wind, and the date and local time at the airport. Every attempt and every restart loads the same ones.

## Fixes
- **A challenge at another airport now starts there.** The sim moved the aircraft back to the runway your flight began on and closed the attempt; a crash then did not restart it. The clean start now departs from the challenge's runway.

## Good to know
- The hold has only been flown in the C172 so far. Keep the wings level yourself: it does not touch the ailerons.
