0.11.4 ties FOQA-Sim to Pilot Aptitude, the site its landing challenges come from. There is no address to type: Pilot Aptitude is already under Challenges → Sites, and you only press Sign in.

## Challenges

- **Pilot Aptitude is built in.** It is in the Sites list from the first start and can't be removed (you can still sign out). "Add site" is gone.
- **“Fly this challenge” works from Pilot Aptitude.** As in 0.11.3, the button on Pilot Aptitude puts you on final once you are signed in. Links from any other website are not opened: FOQA-Sim says it opens challenge links from Pilot Aptitude only.
- **Before challenges open there**, the Sites list says "Challenges aren't open on Pilot Aptitude yet." FOQA-Sim checks again when you open the Challenges screen or press Sign in, and once they are open everything works without a restart or an update.
