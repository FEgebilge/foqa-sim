0.9.0 shows each approach against the profile it was graded by, the way an instructor overlays your path on the ideal one.

## New

- **Approach profile** under every scored landing, with distance to the threshold across the bottom:
  - **Vertical path** against the standard 3° path to 50 ft over the threshold, with your ILS glideslope where one was received.
  - **Speed** above or below your approach speed, with the landing score's own bands.
  - **Sink** against the rate the 3° path needs at your ground speed.
  - **Configuration**: gear and flaps along the approach, with the stabilised gate.
- **Where you left the profile**: the first point each criterion went outside its tolerance, exactly where the landing score found it. Click it to put the replay cursor there.
