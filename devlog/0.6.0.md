0.6.0 lets you share a landing as an image and export your flights to the tools you already use.

## New

- **Share card**: a clean image of a landing with its score, the six assessment areas, sink rate, g, touchdown distance, centreline and wind. Copy it or save it as a PNG.
- **Export**: any flight as CSV (every recorded parameter, with units), KML (the 3D path in Google Earth, coloured by flight phase, with your touchdowns and findings), GPX (for map tools) or Parquet.
- **Little Navmap logbook**: export your flights in the format Little Navmap imports.
- **Private by default**: exports never contain file paths, and the aircraft title and tail number are only included when you tick the box.

## Fixed

- Older exports carried the flight's file path and full aircraft identity; they no longer do.
- The landing gear fix from 0.5.0 is now tied to how a recording was made, so it can never apply twice.
