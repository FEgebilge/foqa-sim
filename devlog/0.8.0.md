0.8.0 looks at the whole flight the way an airline's flight data programme does: how much you hand-flew, how smooth it was for the cabin, how efficiently you came down. These are measures with their sources, not a grade: turbulence, the aircraft and ATC move every one of them.

## New

- **Flying skills** on every debrief, in four parts:
  - **Automation**: autopilot and autothrottle time in each phase, the height you took over on the final approach, and how long you hand-flew to touchdown.
  - **Ride quality**: a cabin comfort measure read against the ISO 2631 comfort bands, side load as the ball shows it, sideslip, peak bank in each phase against the usual 30°, and rotation rate against about 3°/s for airliners.
  - **Fuel and descent**: fuel used, per hour and per nautical mile, taxi fuel, and how long you flew level in the descent (EUROCONTROL's continuous-descent measure).
  - **Control activity**: how often the controls changed direction while you hand-flew.
- **Trends** gain a Flying row: autopilot share, the usual disconnect height (autolands counted apart), ride, level-offs in the descent and fuel per hour for each type.
- `fdm debrief` without `--json` prints the flying skills.

## Changed

- Hops and slews under a minute in the air leave ride, fuel and control out instead of reading as smooth flying.
- The header fits more window sizes: long flight names shorten instead of pushing buttons off the edge.
