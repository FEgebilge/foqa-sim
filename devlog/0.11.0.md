title: Flights that survive, landings judged like an FDM analyst would
---

0.11.0 is a quality release. It keeps your flights when things go wrong, and it makes the analysis read more like an airline's flight data review.

## Recording you can trust

- **A sim crash no longer loses the flight**: if MSFS crashes or is closed from Task Manager, the flight is saved and analysed, and FOQA-Sim waits for the sim again instead of saying "Recording" forever.
- **Quitting during a pause saves the flight** too.
- **No flight while you sit in the main menu**: opening FOQA-Sim with MSFS already in the main menu no longer starts recording; the flight starts when you start one.
- **A full disk or a locked folder** no longer drops the open flight: what was recorded is kept, the tray says which folder could not be written, and FOQA-Sim retries calmly instead of every two seconds.
- **Flights cut off by a crash are recovered** at the next start, up to where the recording ends.
- **One flight, one aircraft**: changing aircraft, loading another flight, or teleporting from the world map now ends the flight and starts a new one.
- **A log file**: FOQA-Sim now keeps a log in its app data folder, and if it cannot start it says why instead of disappearing.

## Landings, judged more fairly

- **Hard landings are measured like a flight data recorder measures them**, at 16 samples a second, so a single noisy sim frame no longer turns a firm landing into a hard one.
- **Taking a high-speed exit is not a runway excursion.** Turning off onto a rapid-exit taxiway at up to 50 kt is normal technique.
- **Light aircraft aim where light aircraft aim**: 150 m past the threshold, not an airliner's 400 m aiming point. Threshold crossing height now compares wheels with wheels.
- **Slew, teleports and flight reloads are not landings**, and they don't spoil the approach that follows them.
- **A displaced threshold** no longer leaves a landing unscored; touching down before it counts as short.
- **Autobrake, reversers and prop beta** count as starting to slow down.
- **A crash during the landing** now scores 0.
- **Heights are measured from the airport you are flying to**, also for a go-around or a low approach somewhere else, and flight phases no longer run across a slew or teleport.
- **Crab angle uses the true heading**, so magnetic variation no longer skews it.

## New and corrected events

- **Rejected takeoffs** are recognised as their own phase (RTO), with the speed they were rejected at.
- **Takeoff events**: rotation rate, flaps retracted too early, and bank close to the ground after liftoff.
- **Gear not down at 500 ft** now applies to every retractable aircraft, not only airliners.
- **Stall warnings** at liftoff and, for airliners, in the flare are no longer ignored.
- **Light-aircraft approaches** get sink-rate and bank events from 1000 ft, not just at 500 ft.
- **One event, not several**: a value hovering at a limit gives one event, and the gear is read the way a recorder stores it.

## More aircraft, judged as what they are

- **Approach speeds for the airliners**: the A320 family, 737, 747-8 and 787 now carry approach speeds from the manufacturers' airport-planning data, so speed-high and speed-low events finally fire for them.
- **New aircraft**: A320ceo, 777-300ER, 777-200, Cessna 152 and Piper Archer.
- **Better recognition**: an aircraft is matched by its type, then by the names add-ons and liveries use, then by what the sim says about it (engines, gear, category, weight). The debrief says how it was recognised, and when no profile matched, which checks were therefore off.
- **Helicopters and gliders** are no longer scored as if they had landed an airliner on a runway.
- **New events**: Mach above Mmo, and landing above the maximum landing weight.

## Lighter next to the sim

- **Far less memory in the tray**: closing the window now frees it; FOQA-Sim waits in the tray using about a tenth of the memory it used to.
- **Less work while you fly**: the in-sim panel's live analysis runs only while the panel is open, the recorder waits for the sim instead of checking a thousand times a second, reads fewer frames in cruise (every frame near the ground), and writes to disk once a second.
- **Re-analysing your flights after an update** runs at background priority, so it doesn't compete with the sim.
- **A faster app**: screens load when you open them, and debrief playback no longer redraws the whole page each step.

## A friendlier app

- **Getting started in four steps**: a fresh install shows the way to your first debrief, in-sim panel, sim connected, fly, debrief, with each step live and the one thing that moves it on.
- **Your latest flight, in context**: the flight list opens with your newest flight and how it compares with your own recent landings in that type, once you have a few.
- **A flight list for hundreds of flights**:
  - Search, filters and scroll are kept when you come back from a debrief.
  - Filter by aircraft, airport, tag and dates.
  - Recordings that were never a flight are hidden.
  - Select many to move to the trash or export.
  - Every delete can be undone; the trash empties itself after 30 days.
- **Titles, notes and tags** for every flight, written in the debrief and searchable in the list.
- **A clearer debrief**:
  - What changes how to read the score comes first, with the events worth a look right under it.
  - Older and newer flight buttons (J / K).
  - Aviation terms explain themselves when you point at them.
  - A tick shows your own median sink rate and load on the gauges.
  - The layout works from the smallest window up, and stays still as you move the pointer over the track and charts.
- **Settings**: fuel in kilograms or pounds, runway distances in metres or feet, UTC or local time, and your date and number format. Altitude and speed stay in feet and knots, as in every cockpit.
- **Easier to read and use**:
  - Stronger colours for the light theme.
  - Notices with a button stay until you close them.
  - Tooltips on every header button.
  - A skip-to-content link, and calmer motion when Windows asks for it.
- **Setup reads as steps**, and says what is required and what is optional, also for MSFS 2020. Challenges show where you are in the flow, and Trends fill wide windows.

## Landing method landing-3

- **The landing assessment is now `landing-3`**: the fixes above change what a landing scores, and a published method never changes. Challenge attempts you already flew keep the method and score they were graded with.

## Smaller fixes

- In-sim, the panel needs reinstalling once from the Setup screen so its live tabs keep working.

- Replacing a challenge with a newer copy now really replaces it.
- A broken link no longer blanks the window; a failing panel shows a message with a way out.
- In the debrief, Space and the arrow keys work on the focused button again instead of moving the playback.
- The app no longer shows one challenge's briefing on another after a quick switch.
