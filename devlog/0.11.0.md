title: Flights that survive, landings judged like an FDM analyst would
---

0.11.0 is a quality release. It keeps your flights when things go wrong, and it makes the analysis read more like an airline's flight data review.

## Recording you can trust

- **A sim crash no longer loses the flight**: if MSFS crashes or is closed from Task Manager, the flight is saved and analysed, and FOQA-Sim waits for the sim again instead of saying "Recording" forever.
- **Quitting during a pause saves the flight** too.
- **A full disk or a locked folder** no longer drops the open flight: what was recorded is kept, the tray says which folder could not be written, and FOQA-Sim retries calmly instead of every two seconds.
- **Flights cut off by a crash are recovered** at the next start, up to where the recording ends.
- **One flight, one aircraft**: changing aircraft, loading another flight, or teleporting from the world map now ends the flight and starts a new one.
- **A log file**: FOQA-Sim now keeps a log in its app data folder, and if it cannot start it says why instead of disappearing.

## Landings, judged more fairly

- **Hard landings are measured like a flight data recorder measures them**, at 16 samples a second, so a single noisy sim frame no longer turns a firm landing into a hard one.
- **Taking a high-speed exit is not a runway excursion.** Turning off onto a rapid-exit taxiway at up to 50 kt is normal technique.
- **Light aircraft aim where light aircraft aim**: 150 m past the threshold, not an airliner's 400 m aiming point. Threshold crossing height now compares wheels with wheels.
- **Slew, teleports and flight reloads are not landings**, and they don't spoil the approach that follows them.
- **A displaced threshold** no longer leaves a landing unscored; touching down before it counts as short.
- **Autobrake, reversers and prop beta** count as starting to slow down.
- **A crash during the landing** now scores 0.

## New and corrected events

- **Rejected takeoffs** are recognised as their own phase (RTO), with the speed they were rejected at.
- **Takeoff events**: rotation rate, flaps retracted too early, and bank close to the ground after liftoff.
- **Gear not down at 500 ft** now applies to every retractable aircraft, not only airliners.
- **Stall warnings** at liftoff and, for airliners, in the flare are no longer ignored.
- **Light-aircraft approaches** get sink-rate and bank events from 1000 ft, not just at 500 ft.
- **One event, not several**: a value hovering at a limit gives one event, and the gear is read the way a recorder stores it.

## Smaller fixes

- Replacing a challenge with a newer copy now really replaces it.
- A broken link no longer blanks the window; a failing panel shows a message with a way out.
- In the debrief, Space and the arrow keys work on the focused button again instead of moving the playback.
- The app no longer shows one challenge's briefing on another after a quick switch.
