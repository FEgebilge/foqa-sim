title: A new landing score, everywhere at once
---

The landing score is now **landing-4**, the same in the app, the tablet, the in-sim panel, challenges and the leaderboards. Your past flights are scored again with it, so their grades may change.

## It says what it could not measure
- A flight that stayed below the approach window used to be graded against the wrong speed without a word (a C172 against 52 kt). The speed now comes from your flaps as you landed, and anything that could not be graded is listed on the landing.
- A ranked attempt with missing data does not count.

## Fairer limits
- **Touchdown speed, light aircraft:** touching down near the stall now loses points. The stall speed follows your weight.
- **Very hard landings:** without a published limit from the manufacturer, a touchdown past the top level for its class (2.6 g for light aircraft) is held to 39.
- **Approach speed, light aircraft:** anywhere in the handbook's range is ideal.
- **Touchdown point, light aircraft:** you aim at the runway's own aiming point, not 150 m on every runway.
- **Drift** is graded as an angle, so it no longer depends on your speed.
- **A small skip** is no longer a bounce, and a flat touchdown is no longer graded as nose first.
- **Jets and turboprops:** touchdown speed allows for the wind additive.

## Aircraft data
- **737-800:** Vref from the Boeing QRH for flaps 15, 30 and 40.

## Good to know
- Many limits are our own estimates, and they are marked as such in the method. Jet and turboprop limits have only been tried on synthetic flights.
- A310, ATR 72, Bonanza, PC-12, PC-24 and Saab 340 still have no reference speed: speed items are not graded on them, and the landing says so.
