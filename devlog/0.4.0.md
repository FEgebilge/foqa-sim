0.4.0 brings landing challenges into the simulator and rewrites every assessment text in plain language.

## New

- **Challenge status in the sim**: the toolbar panel and the EFB show the attempt as it goes: placed, gate, landed, graded, and the result. **Fly again** starts a new attempt on the same challenge without leaving the sim.
- **EFB Challenges tab**: your challenge library, the briefing with the pre-flight check, and Place, all on the tablet.
- **Challenge links from websites**: a site that supports the open FOQA challenge format can link to a challenge. FOQA-Sim always asks before it does anything with a link, and you can turn links off.
- **Notifications** when a connected site scores an attempt, which you can also turn off.
- **Plain-language assessment**: banners, deductions and notes now read like a debrief, for example "Descent rate 1,565 fpm above 300 ft holds this landing below Good".

## Fixed

- The touchdown point says "before" or "past" the target correctly.
- Several robustness fixes in how challenge links and in-sim commands are handled.
