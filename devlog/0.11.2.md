0.11.2 changes what counts as a go-around, after a circuit flight with a go-around and a touch-and-go showed one missed and one that never was.

## Go-arounds

- **A go-around from high on final is found.** Going around from 800 ft or higher, the climb passed 1000 ft before it had risen enough to count, so the flight showed no go-around at all. The climb is now followed past 1000 ft, as long as it lasts.
- **A dive away from the field is not a go-around.** An approach now has to close on a runway: a descent below 1000 ft and climb while flying away from every runway stays en route, even near an airport recorded without runways, such as a heliport.
- **Levelling off at circuit height is not one either**: the approach has to come down by at least 200 ft first, to more than 50 ft under 1000 ft.
- **The in-sim panel agrees**: its live phases use the same rule. A go-around from high on final shows there once its climb has risen 200 ft.

Go-arounds and the phases around them may change in flights you already recorded; they are analysed again after the update.
