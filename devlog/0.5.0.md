0.5.0 turns the debrief into a flight data replay and fixes how the landing gear is read.

## New

- **Replay**: the map, a reconstructed primary flight display and the charts follow one time slider. Play at 1× or 4×, or jump to any finding with `[` and `]` or a click on its card.
- **Primary flight display**: attitude, airspeed with the flap and maximum speed bands of your aircraft type, altitude with radio height near the ground, vertical speed, heading, flaps, gear and autopilot. Values the recorder doesn't measure directly are marked †, and a missing value reads "no data" instead of being made up.
- **Smaller debriefs**: flight data is sent rounded to what is shown, so a debrief opens with about a sixth less data.

## Fixed

- **Landing gear**: the simulator reports gear extension on a 0–1 scale where FOQA-Sim expected percent. Aircraft with retractable gear could be flagged "gear not down at 500 ft" on every approach, and gear overspeed was never caught. New recordings are read correctly and old ones are corrected when they are opened.
