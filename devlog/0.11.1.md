0.11.1 fixes the flight list that some of you could not open after 0.11.0, and lets you look at what you flew by hand apart from what the autopilot flew.

## Fixes

- **The flight list opens again.** Ending a flight back to the main menu, or a reload or slew after a landing, could leave a flight that stopped the whole list from loading ("Could not read the flights"). Those flights now analyse normally and come back in the list.
- **One bad flight no longer hides the others**: if a flight still cannot be analysed, it is left out and the rest of the list shows.
- **The in-sim panel's last landing no longer flickers**: a touchdown that the flight's analysis later drops, such as a wheel hop on the takeoff roll, stops showing instead of coming and going every few seconds.

## Autopilot or hand-flown

- **Autopilot strip on the timeline**: under the phase bar you see where the autopilot was engaged, and a tick marks where it came off before the landing.
- **Events say how you were flying**: each event is tagged AP or Hand-flown, and the list can show only one of them.
- **Ride quality, split**: switch the card between the whole flight, the part on the autopilot and the part flown by hand.
- **"Hand-flown" instead of a dash**: when the autopilot was off through the whole approach, the Automation card says so, and Hand-flown final shows the approach's time.

## Replay

- **Flight display, map and charts in view together**: on a wide window the flight display sits over the map, and the charts get the wide column beside them, so one cursor shows where you were, what the instruments read and every parameter at once. The events list moved into a tab next to the charts.
