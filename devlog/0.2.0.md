0.2.0 explains how every flight is graded and splits it into phases the way airline flight data monitoring does.

## New

- **How this was graded**: the debrief and the EFB break the grade down rule by rule, with the points each one brings and where the flight sits on the Good / Fair / Poor scale.
- **Flight phases after ICAO definitions**: phase boundaries follow your aircraft. A light aircraft flares at 20 ft, turboprops such as the PC-12 start the approach at a 1500 ft pattern, and a touch-and-go gets its own takeoff.
- **Why a phase started**: hover a phase on the timeline to see what opened it, for example "descending through 1000 ft above the aerodrome". A panel lists the values in effect and where they come from.
- **Wind at touchdown**: the landing card shows the wind and its components on the runway: headwind or tailwind, and crosswind from which side.
- **What's new**: after an update, the app shows these notes once. All notes stay under the newspaper icon.
- **New icon** in the toolbar and on the EFB: a recorded parameter breaking its limit, flagged at the peak.

## Fixed

- The stall warning no longer counts as a finding in a light aircraft's flare.
- Several robustness fixes in how recordings are read and checked.
