0.3.0 replaces the points-based grade with a landing assessment built on the tolerances examiners use and the thresholds airline flight data monitoring uses, and adds local landing challenges.

## New

- **Landing assessment out of 100**: every landing is graded across six domains: approach stability, threshold and flare, touchdown point, lateral control, touchdown contact and rollout. Each measurement is read against checkride tolerances (FAA ACS) and FDM thresholds (EASA Data4Safety, FSF ALAR), and the debrief shows which item set each deduction.
- **Safety caps**: a continued unstable approach, a landing that needs a hard-landing inspection or leaving the runway caps the score, with a banner that says why.
- **Data you can trust**: when a measurement is missing (no radio height, a data gap), that item is not assessed rather than given full marks, and the result is marked Partial.
- **Landing challenges (local)**: import a challenge file, check the pre-flight card, place the aircraft on final with one click and fly again as often as you like. Every attempt keeps its own log and result.
- **Approach speeds** from the type's own documents where we could source them (Cessna 172S first); types without a source are not judged on speed.
- **EFB**: the score, grade, six mini bars and the top deduction for every flight.

## Fixed

- A touch-and-go at the end of a recording no longer counts as leaving the runway.
- Lowering the gear late between 1000 and 500 ft is graded as a late configuration, not as a gear-up approach.
