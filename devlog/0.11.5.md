0.11.5 lets a challenge author try a draft in the sim before publishing it, and makes a wrong runway name easy to fix.

## Challenges

- **A wrong runway names the right ones.** When a challenge asks for a runway the sim does not have, the message now lists those it has: "LTFJ has no runway 06 in the sim, only 06L, 24R".
- **Test flights.** A Pilot Aptitude admin can open a draft challenge from the site and fly it in the sim before publishing. It is flown as practice and never reaches a leaderboard.
