0.10.0 is the first version anyone can download from GitHub, and the last one you install by hand: from here on FOQA-Sim updates itself.

## New

- **Automatic updates**: when a new version is out, FOQA-Sim tells you with its release notes. Choose **Install and restart** and it updates in a small window and opens again. It never installs while a flight is being recorded.
- **Check for updates** on the What's new screen, whenever you like.
- **Signed updates**: every update is signed with the project's key, and the app refuses one that does not match.
- **Download page**: the installer is on GitHub, with its SHA-256 in each release.

## Note

- The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
