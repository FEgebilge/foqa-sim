---
version: 0.11.3
date: 2026-10-30
title: Localizer deviations only for the runway you fly to
---

0.11.3 fixes localizer and glideslope findings on approaches flown without that runway's ILS, after a visual approach landed 3 m off the centreline was reported 2 dots off the localizer all the way down.

## ILS deviations

- **Deviations count only when you land on the runway the ILS belongs to.** With another runway's ILS tuned (a parallel runway, the opposite end, another airport) the needles still move, often to full scale, but say nothing about your approach, so localizer and glideslope findings are left out there, as a real FDM program does.
- **How FOQA-Sim tells**: the recorder now records the localizer's course. The ILS belongs to the runway you landed on when that course points along the runway and the localizer needle is near the middle as you touch down on the centreline. The opposite end points the other way; a parallel runway's localizer points the same way but reads near full scale when you are on this runway's centreline. The sim does not report reliably which airport and runway the ILS serves, so the course and the needle are what is used.
- **If you retune on the way**, only the part flown on the landing runway's ILS counts.
- **Flights recorded before 0.11.3** have no localizer course, so their localizer and glideslope findings are left out entirely: no finding is better than a wrong one. The same goes for the approach before a go-around, which lands on no runway to compare with.

Localizer and glideslope findings may disappear from flights you already recorded; they are analysed again after the update.

## Challenges

- **Fly again starts with an undamaged aircraft.** The first Place of a challenge (or the first Fly again with none saved) saves the start as a clean flight; every Fly again loads it back, waits for the sim to settle on the runway, then places you at the start, so a crash or damage never carries into the next attempt. It takes about 15 seconds and is shown in the attempt log. The sim does not report damage, so this happens every time, and only with the same aircraft type as the saved start.
- **A challenge flight now starts at the challenge start.** Placing you on final used to keep recording into the flight you were in, so its debrief began on the runway with a takeoff and a climb that never happened and the jump to final. Now the placement ends that flight where you were (kept as its own flight, or dropped if it was only a few seconds) and starts a new one on final; the attempt, its debrief and its analysis all use that one.
- **Wing, propeller and nose strikes are crashes.** A landing that ends with a wingtip or the propeller on the runway (a ground loop or nose-over after touchdown, or touching down with a wing too low) no longer counts and no longer scores: the attempt ends as a crash and Fly again starts by itself, the attempt log says what struck, the grade is held at zero and the debrief shows it as a high-level event. FOQA-Sim tells it from the attitude on the ground (more than 12° of bank in a light aircraft, 8° in an airliner, or the nose more than 5° down, for half a second), since the sim reports no crash to other apps.
- **Every challenge is graded the way the debrief grades the landing.** A challenge file may name an older scoring method (landing-1, landing-2); it is now always graded with the current landing assessment, so the attempt's score and the debrief's never disagree. The briefing shows the method actually used.
- **A crash flies you again by itself.** The sim does not report a crash to other apps, so FOQA-Sim reads the impact: a touchdown at 4 g or 2,000 fpm or more ends the attempt and starts reloading the clean start a second after the impact; you are back on final about 15 seconds later.
- **No other aircraft during a challenge.** Every attempt now starts from the same saved start, with the sim's AI air traffic off, so no airliner crosses your final and leaves its wake behind. The first attempt takes about 15 seconds longer to start.
- **Signing in to a challenge site works with sites that route in the page address** (like Pilot Aptitude): the sign-in request now reaches the page.
- **“Fly this challenge” on your own site puts you on final.** From a site you are signed in to, the app no longer asks or stops at the briefing: it takes the challenge, places the aircraft and starts the ranked attempt. If something is red in the pre-flight check (no flight loaded, another aircraft), it shows the briefing with what to fix. Links from other sites still ask first.
- **A challenge finishes when you stop.** Once the aircraft has come to a stop after the landing (under 2 kt for 3 seconds), the attempt is graded and uploaded at once; no need to press Finish or wait two minutes.
