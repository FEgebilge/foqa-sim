title: Trends count flights, not recordings
---

0.10.1 makes Trends count what an airline counts: flights flown, not every time the sim was open.

## Changed

- **Only real flights count**: a recording counts as a flight when the aircraft spent at least a minute and a mile in the air at flying speed. A takeoff or a landing is not needed, so a challenge that starts on final or a flight you ended in the air still counts.
- **What was left out is said plainly**: under the figures, Trends tells you how many recordings were not counted and why (never left the ground, or too short to be a flight), and how many counted flights started or ended in the air.
- **Hours flown**: the hours are now time in the air, not time with the sim open.
- **Slew is not flying**: in Trends, time moved in slew mode no longer counts as time in the air.
