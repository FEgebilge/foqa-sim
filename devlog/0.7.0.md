0.7.0 adds what an airline safety department looks at first: not one flight, but the pattern across all of them.

## New

- **Trends**: a new screen that reads every flight you have recorded. Filter by aircraft, airport and period.
- **Landing score over time**: each landing as a dot coloured by grade, with a running average. Click a dot to open that flight.
- **Touchdown footprint**: where your wheels touched, past the threshold and off the centreline, drawn on the runway with its touchdown zone and aiming point.
- **Landing areas**: which of the six assessment areas costs you points most often.
- **Event rates**: how many flights in every 100 had each event, the way airlines quote rates per 1,000 sectors, and whether your newer flights are better or worse than your older ones. With fewer than 5 flights on either side the screen says so instead of drawing a trend.
- **By aircraft and by airport**: score, touchdown zone share and centreline offset for each.
- `fdm trends`: the same report on the command line.
- **Always one rule set**: after an update every flight is analysed again from its recording, so trends never mix old and new results.

## Changed

- The header fits small windows: on narrow screens the buttons show icons only.
