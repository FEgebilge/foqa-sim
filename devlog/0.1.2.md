0.1.2 fixes a recording bug and gathers what has been added since 0.1.0.

## Fixed

- **Start new flight** now works after **End & save**: the recorder starts a new flight instead of staying idle.

## New since 0.1.0

- **Limits for 22 aircraft types**: rules use each type's own speeds and pitch limits, so Airbus, Boeing, turboprops and light aircraft are judged against their own numbers.
- **Crosswind and tailwind checks**: a landing beyond the type's demonstrated crosswind or tailwind limit is flagged.
- **Bounced landings grouped**: a landing and its bounces are one landing (TD1, TD1b, ...), not several.
- **Delete flights** from the flights list, with a confirmation.
- **EFB charts**: the in-sim EFB app draws the flight profile and approach charts for a flight.
